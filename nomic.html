Methodology

Equity Curve

Current Holdings

Symbol% of PortfolioSharesFirst EnteredAverage Entry PriceCurrent PriceP&L %Max DD %MFE %Win

Holdings are the positions the book currently carries, one row per instrument. % of Portfolio is the position's share of the book; Shares is the number held; Average Entry Price is the cost-weighted average paid; P&L % is the current price versus that average; Max DD % and MFE % are the worst drawdown and best gain of the position since first entry.

This Week's Actions

When the set of positions changes, the book rebalances: positions leaving are sold, then the remaining and new positions are set to their target weights. Sells are listed first, then buys, then resizes. If nothing changes, no trades are made.

Every row is one completed position, from entry to exit. Return % is the exit price versus the entry price; P&L $ is the realized dollars on an average-cost basis.
